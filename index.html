💡 5 High-Impact Tips to Travel Greener

Simple everyday habits that save fuel, lower travel costs, and dramatically reduce greenhouse gas emissions across your daily commutes.

Adopt Active Travel

For short trips under 3 km, walking or cycling produces 0 direct emissions, burns calories, and avoids parking hassle completely.

Choose Metro & Bus

Electrified urban metro and buses carry dozens of commuters simultaneously, slashing per-capita emissions by up to 85% compared to solo driving.

Carpool When Driving

Sharing rides with 2–3 colleagues or neighbours divides fuel consumption and emissions proportionally per person, while reducing road congestion.

Maintain Tire Pressure

Keeping vehicle tires properly inflated improves fuel economy by 3–4% and reduces engine strain, cutting unnecessary fuel burn.

Combine Your Errands

Batching multiple small errands into one combined circuit keeps the engine warm, which is significantly more fuel-efficient than several cold-start trips.
